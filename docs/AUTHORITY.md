# Authority boundary

AUTOPILOT computes and coordinates. It does not become an accounting, payment, blockchain or host authority.

`COMPUTATION != AUTHORIZATION != PAYMENT != SETTLEMENT != ACCOUNTING != BLOCKCHAIN FINALITY`

- `AI_CFO` may calculate budget/unit economics and issue proposals; `synergy_financial_os` remains canonical financial authority.
- `AI_SMM`, `AI_SALES`, `AI_VIDEO` may create local artifacts without approval. Publishing/messaging/CRM writes/ads mutations/paid render requests require approval and `AUTOPILOT_EXTERNAL_EFFECTS=1`.
- payment, accounting-booking, blockchain-submit and arbitrary host-shell capabilities are hard-denied.
